Inv. 02

University in Bast Shoes

Location
Leo Tolstoy Yasnaya Polyana Museum-Estate
Opened
October 2025
Role
Unity Team Lead
Built with
Unity, C#, touchscreen, physical interfaces

An interactive information system for the new Centre for Tolstoyan Pedagogy, in the Volkonsky House — touchscreen content about the estate and its teaching tradition, with educational game sections built into it. The audience is preschool and primary-school children — the harshest usability test there is. They press everything, in every order, and they never read instructions. Every interaction had to be understandable without text and impossible to get stuck in.

Exhibition design: Metaforma Museum Bureau · Unity development: Ilya Voitov

02 What I take responsibility for

Exhibits that hold up in public

Twelve hours a day, unattended, in front of thousands of visitors who press every control in an order nobody anticipated. The build has to survive that with no one standing next to it.

Hardware wired into software

Buttons, sensors, encoders, controllers and physical models driving Unity, and Unity driving them back. Commissioning on site, with the contractors, until it actually works in the room.

Games and simulators, not just screens

Touchscreen kiosks with games built into them, and simulators that hand the visitor the controls — interaction models a first-time user works out without being told.

Curators editing their own exhibits

Backend-driven content and custom CMS tools, so the museum changes text, media and languages without calling a developer. It is the difference between a project and an asset.

Architecture for a whole exhibition

Shared frameworks and conventions across dozens of installations, so a team can work in parallel and the hundredth exhibit costs less than the tenth.

Leading the Unity side

Technical specifications, code review, estimates and delivery planning — and writing the difficult modules myself.
